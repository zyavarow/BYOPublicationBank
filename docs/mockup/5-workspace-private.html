What gene therapy approaches show the most promise for GENE3-associated multisystem proteinopathy?
Search
Study Type Mutations Model System Techniques ✓ Reagents
Answering as Patient / Caregiver Clinician ✓ Scientist / Researcher Advocacy Leader draft roles — to workshop
Answer
Confidence: High 12 papers · 48 sec

Current approaches fall into three groups: gene replacement carried by an AAV vector, antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) that dial down the faulty GENE3 transcript, and small molecules that help the cell's own cleanup pathways work better [1][2][3].

Early results suggest ASOs give more precise control over dosing, while gene replacement still struggles to reach every affected tissue [2][4].
