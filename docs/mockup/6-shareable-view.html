What gene therapy approaches show the most promise for GENE3-associated multisystem proteinopathy?
Search
Study Type Mutations Model System Techniques ✓ Reagents
Answering as ✓ Patient / Caregiver Clinician Scientist / Researcher Advocacy Leader draft roles — to workshop
Answer
Confidence: High 12 papers · 48 sec

Researchers are trying a few different ways to fix the problem at its source. One delivers a working copy of the gene straight into cells. Another uses a treatment that quiets down the faulty instructions the gene is sending. A third helps the cell clean up and recycle the buildup this gene problem causes [1][2][3].

So far, the "quieting down" approach seems easier for doctors to fine-tune the dose on, while delivering a whole new gene copy is still hard to get to every part of the body that needs it [2][4].
