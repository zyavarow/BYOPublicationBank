Which of the flagged papers should stay in the bank?

The system flagged these papers while preparing the bank: look-alikes, possible duplicates, papers that are not research, and possible off-topic papers. By default, look-alikes, papers that are not research and possible off-topic papers are set aside (left out of the bank), while possible duplicates and "worth a look" papers stay in until you exclude them. Choose a tab, then use the button on a paper to overrule the default: Keep in bank for set-aside papers, Exclude for the others. Undo puts it back to the default. Your choice is saved straight away, and nothing is ever deleted.
