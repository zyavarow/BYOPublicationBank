A paper passes if it names the disease (any term below) in its title or abstract, or at least three times in the text. Edit the terms, preview what would be flagged, then apply.

Smarter check by AI — costs money

Claude reads each paper's title and abstract against your description of the disease. It also catches look-alikes that share a name with the disease, which the word check cannot. It marks papers on topic, worth a look, or off topic, with a one-line reason.
