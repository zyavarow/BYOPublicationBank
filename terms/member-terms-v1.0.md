## How answers work

Your Organization chooses the papers in its publication bank. AI reads each paper and pulls out information into fields your Organization sets up. When you ask a question, the search finds relevant passages and AI writes an answer with a citation for each statement and a confidence indicator.

Answers only draw on the papers in your Organization's publication bank, and they say when the library doesn't cover part of a question. They can still be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Please check anything important against the cited papers, which remain the authority.
