## Who we are

BYO Publication Bank is run by Zaris Advising LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Zaris," "we," "us"). This notice covers the version of BYO Publication Bank that Zaris hosts. Zaris decides how this information is used and is responsible for it. Today, the only person at Zaris with access to it is Zaris's founder.

For privacy questions or requests, write to zollie@zarisadvising.com.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, role (admin or member), which organizations you belong to, and when you last signed in.
- **Sign-in records:** when sign-in, invitation and password reset links are requested and used, including the network (IP) address and email address of each request. We use these to limit repeated requests and keep accounts secure.
- **Your questions and answers:** each question you ask, the answer and sources you received, the search depth, and the time and cost of the answer.
- **Feedback:** ratings and comments you send about answers, with the related question and answer.
- **Agreement records:** which version of the Member Terms or Organization Agreement you accepted, and when.
- **Access requests:** if you ask for access to create a publication bank, your name, email and what you tell us about your organization.
- **Organization uploads:** papers and other files your Organization uploads. Papers include authors' names, and onboarding transcripts or notes may include the names and words of people on those calls.

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, for up to 14 days. We don't use advertising cookies, analytics tools or tracking across other sites.

## How we use it

- to sign you in, run the service and show your Organization its workspace
- to keep the service secure and prevent misuse
- to support your Organization and fix problems
- to improve BYO Publication Bank, including by comparing how it works across organizations
- to use de-identified questions and aggregate findings in talks, case studies, papers and grant reports, where no person or organization can be identified
- to keep a record of who accepted which terms

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We won't name your Organization or quote you publicly without consent.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see your own account and questions.
- **Your Organization's admins** can see your account details, your questions with your name, the answers you received, your usage and cost, and any feedback you send. They can download the question log.
- **Other members** of your Organization can't see your questions.
- **Zaris** can see all of the above, plus sign-in records and access requests.
- **Our service providers** receive only what they need to run the service. They're listed on the Service Providers page.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or the security of the service.

## Where it's processed

Zaris is based in the United States, and your information is processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while your Organization uses BYO Publication Bank, until it's deleted on request. When you or your Organization ask us to delete it, we do so within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed as those backups are replaced.

We keep the record of which terms you accepted even after your account is deleted, as proof of what was agreed.

## Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your information
- delete your information

Write to zollie@zarisadvising.com or ask your Organization's admin. We respond within 30 days.

## If you're in the European Union or United Kingdom

We use your information on these legal bases:

- **To provide the service** you've signed up for: your account, sign-in, questions and answers.
- **Our legitimate interests:** keeping the service secure, supporting organizations, improving the software, and using de-identified or aggregate findings in our work. We've weighed these against your interests, and you can object to them.
- **Legal obligations:** keeping records the law requires.

You also have the right to:

- restrict how we use your information
- object to uses based on our legitimate interests
- receive information you gave us in a format you can move to another service
- complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Your information is processed in the United States. Where we send it there, we rely on our service providers' safeguards for international transfers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Health information

Please don't put identifiable health information into questions or feedback. If you do, we'll treat it as confidential, and you can ask us to delete it.

## Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypted storage of API keys, sign-in links that work once and expire, and access limited to people who are approved or invited. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell your Organization and, where the law requires, you and the relevant authorities.
