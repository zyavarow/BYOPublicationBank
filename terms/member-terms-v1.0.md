## Member Terms (beta)

BYO Publication Bank is in **beta**. It is a tool for exploring a library of scientific papers that your organization has loaded. It may contain errors, change without notice, or be unavailable.

### Research use only

- Answers are AI-generated summaries of the papers in your organization's publication bank. They are for research and information only.
- Answers are **not medical advice**, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a qualified clinician about any health decision.
- Always check an answer against the cited sources before you rely on it.

### Please do not enter identifiable health information

- Do not type names, dates of birth, contact details, medical record numbers or other identifiable health information about yourself or anyone else into a question.

### Acceptable use

- Use the service only for your organization's legitimate research and advocacy purposes.
- Do not try to break, overload or get around the service, and do not share your sign-in links with other people.

### Feedback

- If you send feedback, we may use it to improve the service.

### Beta access

- Access is by invitation and may be withdrawn at any time.
